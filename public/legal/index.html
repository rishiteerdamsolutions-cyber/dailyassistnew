Last updated: June 3, 2026

  • Payment data: Payments are processed by our payment partners (e.g. Razorpay, PayU, Cashfree). We do not store full card numbers or UPI PINs on our servers.
  • Local data on your computer: AHA stores settings under ~/.aha/ (or the Windows equivalent), local working files as needed for your tasks, and optional API keys in local config. Routine assistance does not send your screen content to our servers for AI generation.
  • Screen and vision: When you ask AHA to help with a task that uses vision, the desktop app may capture screenshots of your display (or browser region) and, if you configured one, send them to your chosen AI provider using your API key. We do not use your API keys on our servers. You can revoke Screen Recording and Accessibility permissions in your OS settings at any time.
