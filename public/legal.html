Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

This policy describes how dailyassist.xyz and the AHA desktop app handle information.

  • Account data: If you sign in, we use Firebase Authentication (email, Google sign-in) and may store your user profile in Supabase (user id, email, license status). This is used to activate your license and optional cloud vault features.
  • Payment data: Payments are processed by our payment partners (e.g. Razorpay, PayU, Cashfree). We do not store full card numbers or UPI PINs on our servers.
  • Local data on your computer: AHA stores settings under ~/.aha/ (or the Windows equivalent), content vault files under your Downloads folder, and BYOK API keys in local config unless you use OS keychain integration when available. Tier-1 social posting does not send your posts to our servers for AI generation.
  • Screen and input data (Tier-2): When you ask AHA to help with a task that uses vision, the app may capture screenshots of your display (or browser region) and send them to your chosen AI provider using your API key. We do not use your BYOK key on our servers for Tier-2. You can revoke Screen Recording and Accessibility permissions in your OS settings at any time.
  • Cloud vault (optional): If enabled, media metadata and files may sync to Supabase Storage under your account, protected by row-level security.
  • Cookies (website): dailyassist.xyz may use cookies for sign-in sessions and analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser.
  • We do not sell your personal information to data brokers.
  • Your rights: You may request access, correction, or deletion of account data we hold by emailing support@dailyassist.xyz.
