FAQ

What is AHA?

Daily assistance on your desktop computer. You ask in chat; it helps on your screen on Mac or Windows.

Does AHA work on mobile?

No. AHA is desktop software only — Mac and Windows. There is no iPhone, iPad, or Android app. You can browse this website on a phone to subscribe, but you install and use AHA on a computer.

Is this automation software?

No. AHA is an assistant that helps when you ask. It runs on your machine; you stay in control.

Do I need my own AI API key?

No to get started. Some tasks can optionally use a key you add in the desktop app settings — your provider, your cost.

Mac and Windows desktop?

Yes. One subscription. Download the Mac or Windows desktop installer from the Download page — not from an app store on your phone.
