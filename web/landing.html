FAQ

Common questions

Do I need any technical skills to use AHA?
No. You install the Chrome extension (one-time, takes 2 minutes), enter your license key, and click Run. Everything else is automated. Your content team handles the Content Engine packaging.
Is AHA safe? Will platforms ban my account?
AHA is designed to be undetectable — it uses real mouse curves, natural typing, and actual browser interaction. However, no automation tool provides a 100% guarantee against platform detection. Use it responsibly with realistic posting frequency.
Where does my content (photos, videos, text) go?
Nowhere. Everything stays on your own computer in a local folder (~/Downloads/aha/). AHA reads files directly from your disk. We never see or store your content.
How does the "first month free" work?

Can I use AHA on multiple computers?
